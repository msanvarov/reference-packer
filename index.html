For AI video on Higgsfield

One subject. One sheet.

Pack every angle of a character, product or place into a single clean reference image. It costs one reference slot, keeps lighting and scale consistent, and gives the model the whole subject at once. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Hailuo, Grok and Kling 2.6 only take a start frame, and a sheet used as a start frame animates the grid itself. For those, make a still from the sheet with an image model first, then animate that still.
